The idea

Two words, two meanings, one table

"Table" is where guests sit. "Tab" is the bill you open and settle, and it is also the interface tab you tap. The TT monogram is both T's side by side: a shared top and two legs. Read it once and it is a table.

TableQR per table, a shared cart, host and guests, floor map.
TabRunning bill, split by person, tip, pay from the phone, close at the till.
Tab shapeFolder-tab corners as a UI motif: rounded top, flat bottom. Used on chips, nav and the mark's bar.
The TT mark

Eight ways to build it

All drawn from two T's. Concept 1 is my pick: it holds up at 16 px, reads as a table, and has a tidy gap between the letters.

Where this could go wrong

Name crowding. "Tab" is used by many products, including the famous soft drink, and several payment and ordering apps. "Table Tab" is a descriptive pair, which is harder to protect as a trademark. Search the Egyptian and Gulf registries and app stores before you spend on design.
Arabic reach. Your buyers are Arabic-first. "Table Tab" works best as a modern, English-led brand with an Arabic line (تيبل تاب). If most venue owners say the name in Arabic, test that it is easy to say and to remember.
Two brands in your files. The earlier مائدة identity, the rename prompt and the master plan are built on the old name. If you switch, the rename prompt needs rewriting before anyone runs it.

Sketches for direction, not final artwork. A designer should redraw the TT with optical corrections. Nothing here has been checked for trademark or domain availability.
