Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "elmenue") collects when you use our QR menu and ordering service, run for each restaurant ("the restaurant") that uses it. Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

Who is responsible for your data

The restaurant decides why your order data is used and is the controller of its guests' and staff data. We operate the platform for the restaurant as its processor, and are responsible for the platform's own security and for platform-level data (restaurant owner accounts, billing).

What we collect

  • Guests: the name or nickname and avatar you type, your table, the items you order, notes, tips, payment method chosen (cash/card), feedback and ratings you give, and optionally a phone number if you join a loyalty programme. A random guest ID and your language/theme choice are kept in your browser (local storage).
  • Staff: name, role and a hashed PIN or password. Actions such as discounts or voids are logged with the staff member's name.
  • Restaurant owners: name, email, hashed password, plan and billing details.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs for security and abuse prevention, kept for a short period.

We do not see or store card numbers. Card payments, if enabled, are completed on the payment provider's own page.

How we use it

To take and serve orders, show bills, run the restaurant's floor, kitchen and reports, prevent abuse, keep the service secure, and (for owners) manage accounts and invoices. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who receives it

The restaurant you order from; our hosting and database providers ([HOSTING PROVIDER], [DATABASE PROVIDER] - data stored in [REGION]); a payment provider if you pay online; and authorities when required by law.

How long we keep it

Order history is kept for the period the restaurant needs for its accounts and legal duties ([RETENTION PERIOD]). Backups are kept for up to [BACKUP PERIOD] days. You can ask the restaurant (or us) to delete or anonymise your personal details.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use, by writing to [PRIVACY EMAIL] or to the restaurant. Browser data can be cleared in your browser settings at any time.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords and PINs are stored hashed, access is limited by role, and the service is rate-limited against abuse. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you suspect a problem.
