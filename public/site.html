IMPORTANT CONDITION

The lifetime payment is a one-time licence for one venue, with no monthly fee. The first year of service is included. From year 2 the annual service (about 20% of the licence) keeps your system running: cloud hosting, backups, security updates, new features and support. Renewal is optional, and the fee is fixed for the first two years. If a renewal is not paid within 30 days of the due date, the service is paused. Your data is kept and can be exported for 60 days, and you can resume by paying the missed period at the current fee.

Prices start from the amounts shown and depend on your size. Talk to us for an exact quote. Pilot venues get help with setup.

05 / Origin

Built for the way cafes and restaurants really work

TableTab started on a real cafe and restaurant floor: crowded tables, a captain who cannot be everywhere, a kitchen that needs the order now. We built what we wished we had.

We are a small team in Egypt, and every pilot venue talks to the people who build the product. If something does not fit your floor, we change it.
